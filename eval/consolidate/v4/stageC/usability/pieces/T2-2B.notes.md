# Notes on writing the piece

## 1. Wanted or needed, not in the material

- **Why the ghosts have no features.** The material says they are featureless and that the missingness is whole-node, but never says why (for example, cited works outside the collected corpus, or no abstract to embed). A reader outside the field will ask this first. I said only that other papers cite them.
- **What biblion actually does with ghosts today.** The material discusses imputed anchors ("a ghost whose anchor is itself the diffusion average"), zero anchors and a masked variant, but never states which one the current pipeline uses. I had to present them as options, which weakens the "does filling in preserve the link" question: I can't say what the current behaviour is.
- **Any measurement on biblion itself.** Every question ends in "must be measured on biblion". There is no number on whether bridges survive, what the ghost fraction is at degree >= 3, or how many bridges a higher threshold loses. The reviewer's ">=3" suggestion is conditional on a sweep that hasn't happened.
- **Plain definitions** of SPECTER2 (only "768-d, dense, semantic"), HDBSCAN (only its mutual-reachability behaviour), homophily, Dirichlet energy, and "teleport". I glossed homophily as "linked nodes tend to share labels" and the anchor/teleport from the APPNP formula; those glosses are mine, inferred from the material's usage.
- **What "denoise" means in biblion beyond PCA to 100 dimensions.** Whether it is centring only or whitening matters (the material says whitening would change the origin-pull geometry more), but the material does not say which biblion does.
- **Size of the effects.** Um et al.'s low-variance result, Yun et al.'s "steeper" drop and Ferrini et al.'s failure on dense features come without numbers, so the piece can only say "steeper" and "failed".
- **Which "deep bibliographic coupling work"** is meant. Named without a reference, so I left it out.
- **PCFI.** Mentioned as a ready-made way to down-weight ghosts far from any real node, but with too little detail (how much it helps, under what conditions) to use beyond a passing reference; I dropped it for length.

## 2. Hard to use

- **The four listed questions (D1-D4) all show "No answer in the research"**, while the answers sit in an untied TL;DR and Key Findings block further down. It took a while to see that the content existed and was simply not linked to the questions.
- **Source voice is mixed.** Many claims are tagged "the reviewer's reasoning", "synthesis", "judgement" or "inference" rather than a paper. Separating what a source showed from what the review argued, sentence by sentence, was the main labour, and some claims are attributed to a paper (e.g. "(Tang et al. 2015)") when the claim is the review's own scoping statement about all the positive evidence.
- **Morehead et al. contradiction.** The material says PCA-100 "reduces prediction variance across seeds", but its own figures (std 0.62 original, 0.80 with PCA-100) show the opposite. I used only the accuracy result.
- **Yun et al. date.** Listed as 2024 but with arXiv number 2508.01209, which implies a 2025 posting. I kept the year as given.
- **Unreferenced and anonymous sources.** Five sources are bare URLs ("Anonymous n.d."), including the ones behind the APPNP closed form and the impute-then-SVD vs SVD-on-observed contrast. I could state those points but not cite them properly. FSD-CAP 2026 appears only in the gaps list, with nothing about what it is.
- **No second reader.** The whole note is flagged as unchecked, which had to go into the piece as a standing caveat.
- **Repetition.** The same claims appear three or four times (TL;DR, Key Findings, Concepts, Do-not-say), sometimes with slightly different scoping. Working out which wording was the authoritative one took cross-checking.
- **Biblion-specific framing.** The material is written as advice to one pipeline, so turning it into a general piece about diffusion and featureless nodes meant keeping biblion as the worked example rather than abstracting away from it; the material offers no other setting to generalise from.
