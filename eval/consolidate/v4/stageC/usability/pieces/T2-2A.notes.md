# Notes on writing the piece

## 1. Wanted or needed, not found in the material

- Why the featureless nodes have no features. The material calls them "ghosts" with "structural (whole-node) missingness" but never says where they come from. I assumed, without saying so, that they are cited works with no embedding available, and avoided stating the cause.
- Which anchor the pipeline gives ghosts today. Recommendation 1 speaks of "the current imputed-anchor design" and Q1 describes an anchor that "is itself the diffusion average", while Q3 is framed around a "zero-anchored ghost" mechanism "as the question states". I could not tell whether the pipeline uses zeros or an imputed average, so the piece presents both as options.
- Exactly what is in "the pipeline": the order of steps and the dimensions. PCA to 100-d on real anchors, APPNP with alpha = 0.3, then UMAP and HDBSCAN can be pieced together, but the number of propagation steps K, how imputed anchors are computed, and whether diffusion runs in 768-d or 100-d are not given (Q4's factorial lists both "raw 768-d" and "100-d ghost injection").
- Any measurement from the pipeline itself. The only data are the 3,392 vs 1,405 node counts. No variance, co-clustering or distortion results exist, so every question ends as open.
- Effect sizes for the downstream erosion mechanisms (how much variance collapse, how much UMAP/HDBSCAN washout). Only qualitative.
- How many edges or bridges are lost at higher degree thresholds. The trade-off is named but no numbers are given even for the example graph.
- The "deep bibliographic coupling" work cited in Q1 has no reference, so I left it out.
- What the Cora benchmark is (I called it "a standard citation benchmark", which is a guess from general knowledge rather than the material) and what dataset the Rossi 4.12% figure is an aggregate over.
- FSD-CAP (2026) is named in the caveats as a supporting source but never described or cited, so I could not use it.

## 2. Hard to use

- The brief is written for the project's developers: project names (biblion, fallworm), "ghost", "anchored fusion", Q1–Q4 labels and verdict tags all needed translating. Its framing is "what should biblion do next", so a general piece had to extract the general findings from recommendations.
- Ambiguity over current design (zero vs imputed anchor), noted above, ran through every section.
- Some citation links do not match the claims they sit on: the oversmoothing claim attributed to Cai & Wang and Oono & Suzuki links to arXiv 2601.07419, which is neither paper; the APPNP closed form links to 2312.16183, not the APPNP paper. I cited the named authors from the reference list and did not rely on the links.
- A mild inconsistency: Q2 says only "the strongest feature-aware methods (FP, PCFI)" held up past 60%, quoting Rossi that FP was "the only" robust feature-aware method; Recommendation 5 says FP is the only method shown robust past 90%. PCFI is a later paper, so it cannot have been in Rossi's comparison. I kept to Rossi's own claim.
- Undefined terms: MADGap, mu_m in Yi's Theorem 1, O-ALS vs I-SVD, "Sparsity Normalization" (only gestured at). Morehead's result includes standard deviations (0.62 vs 0.80) that cut slightly against "reduces prediction variance", which the text claims; I quoted means only.
- Yun et al. is dated 2024 in the text and reference but carries an arXiv number (2508.01209) implying 2025. I kept the material's year.
- The ~60% crossover is quoted from Rossi but the 70% figure is a share of nodes, while Rossi's is a share of feature entries. The brief compares them directly; I flagged the difference only through the "whole nodes vs entries" point.
