## Gaps stated as known
- **gap.6** (row 47): "The counts carry no record of it" treats the relation between library size and load as settled (none). The note lists that correlation as a gap and reports, secondarily, a positive one (read-depth.5).
- Borderline, **gap.36** (row 24): the 21 and 22 copy genomes are given as part of the copy-number range without the caveat that no source checked them against assembly quality.
- Borderline, **gap.18** (row 40): Zhou 2011's 17.2% is used without noting that its discussion gives 13.1%.
