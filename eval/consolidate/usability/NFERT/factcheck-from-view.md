## Gaps stated as if known

None. gap.1 is disclosed (rows 66–68); gap.2 is stated as open (rows 61, 65), though row 64 leans toward an answer; gap.3 (row 58) and gap.4 (row 52) are stated as absent evidence.

## Do not say

No row says what a Do not say entry warns against. The piece keeps the three emission shares apart (dns.1), does not credit microbial efficiency with a reduction that co-varies with soil and management (dns.2, rows 56–58), does not assert dns.3 of the resident community (rows 45, 60), and explicitly declines to read the substitution null as an answer on nitrogen-use efficiency (dns.4, rows 61, 65).
