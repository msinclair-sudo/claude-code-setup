# Notes on writing piece.md

## 1. Wanted or needed, but not in the material

- **Why N₂O matters.** The material never says why anyone cares about N₂O (greenhouse gas, ozone depletion, agricultural and wastewater emissions). A reader from outside the field needs that motivation; I left it out rather than supply it from memory, so the piece opens on the pathway instead of the stakes.
- **What FBA actually is.** The only description is "stoichiometric LP optimization" versus "rate-equation integration", plus mentions of biomass maximisation and exchange reactions. There is no plain account of the steady-state assumption, what the constraints are, or why FBA has no time dimension. I wrote a minimal explanation from those fragments and avoided "steady state", which the material does not use.
- **Dynamic FBA.** The JSON index labels the Mellbye model "genome-scale dynamic FBA" but the text never explains what makes it dynamic or how it differs from plain FBA. I could not say whether it produces time courses, which matters for the comparison with kinetic models.
- **Nitrifier denitrification and hydroxylamine.** The material names the "hydroxylamine-oxidation pathway" and HAO but never says what hydroxylamine is or how that second N₂O route works. Also no explanation of why an ammonia oxidiser carries NirK and NorB in the first place.
- **Numbers on N₂O from the constraint-based models.** No predicted N₂O fluxes, emission fractions or comparisons with measurements for Perez-Garcia 2014 or Mellbye 2018. The only validation figure (15% deviation over 17 yield ratios) is about yields generally, not N₂O specifically.
- **What Mellbye found, quantitatively.** Only the mechanism (low oxygen from coupled metabolism) is given; no magnitude of the coculture effect.
- **Detail on the kinetic and trait-based models.** For ASMN, electron-competition models (Pan et al. has no year or full reference), cybernetic models and the NDHA model, the material gives one sentence each. Nothing on their parameter demands, data needs, or how well they predict N₂O, so the trade-off paragraph in the piece is thin and partly inferred from the general FBA/kinetic contrast.
- **What "cybernetic" means.** Only "dynamic regulation" is given; no definition.
- **Why Zakem et al. leave out NO.** Stated ("neglecting NO") but not explained.
- **Head-to-head comparisons.** No study comparing FBA against kinetic or trait-based models on the same N₂O data, which is what the "how does this compare" part of the brief really wants.
- **The electron-competition mechanism in denitrifiers.** I wanted to say that N₂O builds up when NosZ loses the competition for electrons, but the material only says these models "predict N₂O accumulation" from competition among the four steps. I kept to that.

## 2. Hard to use

- **Project-internal framing.** The opening callout refers to "H2 §2.4", "Community FBA", "Dual Readout", citation keys and a "verdict" being integrated into a proposal; one bullet mentions "the user's 'community-level pathway gaps' mechanism". None of that means anything to an outside reader, and it had to be stripped out.
- **Verdict language.** "PARTIALLY DEMONSTRATED", "NOT FOUND" and the benchmark list are written as an audit of a claim, not as an account of the field. Useful for the conclusion but needed translating.
- **Inconsistency on the 2016 result.** The text says "NO oxidation to NO₂⁻ decreased N₂O formation by 60%"; the JSON index says "CytP460 reduces N2O by 60%". These are not the same claim. I used the verbatim quote from the text.
- **Mixed confidence.** The caveats say methods and reaction tables for the two key constraint-based papers were partly inaccessible, so the electron-coupling detail "could not be confirmed". It is hard to know how far to trust statements about how those models encode the steps.
- **iGC535 accuracy.** "Over 90% prediction accuracy" and "90.52% accuracy" for growth and oxygen uptake are given in one tangled sentence; it was unclear what was being measured, so I used the model only as evidence of curation effort.
- **Gene and enzyme naming switches** between gene names (*nar/nap*, *nirK/nirS*, *norBC*, *nosZ*) and protein names (Nar/Nap, NirK/NirS, cNor/qNor, NorB, NosZ) without a key. A reader outside the field needs one convention.
- **The pathway as modelled by Zakem et al.** is written NO₃⁻→NO₂⁻→N₂O→N₂, which looks like an error against the four-step pathway until you read "neglecting NO".
- **Classification boundary.** The material notes that dynamic FBA and cybernetic models blur the constraint-based/kinetic line, and classifies by "core engine", but Mellbye is called dynamic FBA in one place and plain constraint-based elsewhere.
- **Incomplete references.** Zakem et al., Yuan et al. and Dahal et al. have abbreviated author lists; the "More is Different" review has no authors at all; Pan et al. and Domingo-Félez & Smets have no full citation.
