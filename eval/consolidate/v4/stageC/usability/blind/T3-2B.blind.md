Errors (quote each sentence):
- "Maximising growth rarely supplies one: FBA with a biomass objective does not naturally predict by-product spillover without extra constraints." Overgeneralised. Biomass-maximising FBA does predict some by-product secretion without added constraints (e.g. fermentation products under anoxia). The claim holds for N₂O in a NosZ-carrying cell, not for by-products in general.

Notes:
- Oversimplified but not counted as an error: "Each becomes its own reaction, drawing electrons from the cell's donor pool (NADH and quinones) and coupled to proton pumping." Nir, Nor and Nos take electrons from cytochrome c and cupredoxins, and Nap and Nor are not themselves electrogenic. Proton translocation happens upstream (NADH dehydrogenase, bc1). That is fine in lumped model terms, but it reads as if every step pumps protons.
- "N₂O is the step that matters here" is loose wording: N₂O is a molecule, not a step.
- "Electron-competition models (Pan et al.)" has no year, and the reference list admits the citation is incomplete.
- The opening sentence ("only the first two have so far been used to show N₂O building up when the chain is cut short") is firm, but the body qualifies it.
- The Zakem paragraph's "This is the community pathway-gap mechanism in working form" is the writer's interpretation. It's reasonable and signalled as a link rather than a finding.
- Strong points: the steady-state explanation, settings attached to numbers, and an explicit cautions paragraph on access and verification.
