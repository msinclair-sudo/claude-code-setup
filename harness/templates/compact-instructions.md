## Compact Instructions

This session holds a node in an Agent Workstream Harness. When summarising, keep verbatim:

- the node name, its parent and children, and the task currently open
- every promise not yet in a record: a held recycle, a seam being watched, a follow-up owed to a child or the lead
- owed sign-offs, unanswered questions from children, and any `--after` wait being relied on
- the last instruction from the lead or the operator that has not been carried out

After compacting, run `harness whoami` before anything else; the records outrank the summary.
