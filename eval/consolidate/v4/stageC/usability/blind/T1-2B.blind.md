Errors (quote each sentence):
- "One version of R² asks whether predictions and observations fall on some straight line, which rewards ranking sites correctly even when every prediction is off by a constant factor." Ordinary R² rewards linear association, not correct ranking (that is a rank correlation); minor imprecision in a key explanatory passage.
- "But broad groups are also more abundant, abundance is the strongest correlate of predictability, and none of these studies separates the two." Overstated: "strongest correlate" is one study's result (Werbin); here it reads as a general finding.
- "In streams, the trait composition of invertebrates, fish and diatoms often lines up with environment more closely, and varies less from place to place, than species composition." Unattributed, and "varies less from place to place" adds a claim beyond the environment-matching finding with no source or access status.
- "By that stricter standard, transfer between surveys mostly failed for both kinds of profile." Generalises from one test (Averill to NEON) to "transfer between surveys".
- Sources list: "Frémont et al. (2022) *Nature Climate Change* 12:393–401." is listed but never cited in the text; the stream studies discussed in the text have no entries.
