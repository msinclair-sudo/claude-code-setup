## extraction.8 (claim)

The size of the extraction effect is contested: the review sets Fouhy 2016 (two extraction methods gave very similar data) against Albertsen 2015, Brooks 2015 and MBQC, and the critic counts the claim that, for relative abundance, extraction is the largest step, followed by PCR and then sequencing, as contested by Fouhy 2016 and qualified by MBQC (specimen type mattered more than extraction).
