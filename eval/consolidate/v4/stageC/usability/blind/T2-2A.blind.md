Errors (quote each sentence):
- "The features were sparse word-count vectors." Rossi et al. (2022) also ran OGBN-Arxiv (dense 128-d averaged word embeddings) and OGBN-Products (dense PCA-reduced features), so not all benchmark features were sparse word counts. Minor.
- "Yun, S. et al. (2024). Oldie but goodie: re-illuminating label propagation on graphs with partially observed features. arXiv:2508.01209." An arXiv 2508 ID dates the preprint to August 2025, so the 2024 year in text and list is inconsistent. Minor (bibliographic).
- "Yi's result means any step that changes vector magnitudes, as PCA's centring does, will interact with the shrinkage zeros cause." Overstated: Yi et al. studied sparsity bias in non-graph networks; the consequence for PCA-then-diffusion is an inference stated as a firm implication of their result. Logged as overstatement (counted under A2, not A1).
