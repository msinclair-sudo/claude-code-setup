# Tier 4 narrated animation: what the loop did

**Third version (2026-10-21), after the owner's review of the second:** "the pacing is still way too fast"; "the language is also too dense"; "the animations are bad, but the visuals are good: everything uses the same fade in, fade out"; slow down on key facts such as copy number; keep coming back to the soil; "common" and "rare" used without care. Two research runs first: NP-01 (pacing, wording, structure and motion; vault `Apps/Research`) and MB-02 (16S copies per genome and per cell, abundance and rarity terms, soil abundance; vault `Topics/Biology/Research`). Now 35 segments in 7 chapters, each opening on a title card and closing on a distil line, 7 min 33 s at 1080p30.

- **Pacing:** 2 s after each segment, 3 s after a distil line, 3.5 s at a chapter break, 0.7 s between sentences (was 0.6, none, 1.6, 0.5).
- **Wording:** spoken sentences of about 20 words; "we" and "you"; "abundant" and "low-abundance" for counts in one sample (MB-02: the field splits on "rare", so avoid it); taxa defined when first said; copy number in two segments (per genome, 1 to about 15, rarely more; per cell, from one to dozens in cultures, barely measured in soil); real-soil evidence for the long tail (2% of taxa, about 41% of reads).
- **Motion:** fades went from 119 of 170 animations to 30 (exits only). Things that become other things are transformed (a cell's DNA into a ring, the same bar chart reshaped at each chapter's end); pictures are drawn up (Create, Write, GrowFromEdge); reads travel along paths into the sequencer; the camera pushes into the droplet.
- **Blind fact-check** (../factcheck.md): new and changed claims all supported; one source note tightened.
- **Round 1 reader**, blocking: a label caught mid-write (s3, the segment's animations outran its narration). Fixed, and the tool changed: stills are now taken at the end of each segment's pause, the settled picture. Minor fixes: the soil icon returns in chapters 6 and 7; "missed" for "never drawn"; chart titles state the point.
- **Round 2 reader**, blocking: the y-axis label ran into the soil icon (s27); the real-soil card said "look like this" over a cells chart while the survey counted reads (s3b). Fixed and checked by eye on the round 3 stills (no third reader round).
- **Left as minor:** the soil is off screen from extraction to the count table (chapters 2 to 5); sample read counts drawn in the bins do not follow depth (s18); a few auto-scaled groups (crowd of cells in s2 at 0.60); the s13 title describes rather than states.
- **Tooling:** 30 fps delivery and two chapters rendered at once (a full build 6 min, from about 15); `x build --only` re-renders chosen chapters.

Earlier versions: v1 (12 segments, from memory) skipped every step between sequencing and taxonomic assignment; v2 (21 segments, from MB-01) was accurate but fast, dense and all fades.
