# Notes on writing piece.md

Source used: the single file in this folder, "Stepwise Denitrification with N2O Output in Constraint-Based Metabolic Models.md". Nothing else.

## 1. Things I wanted or needed and could not find in the material

- **Why N₂O matters.** The material never says why anyone cares whether a model reports N₂O (for example, its climate or environmental significance). An outside reader needs a reason, so I fell back on "it is the product when the chain stops short", which is supported, and left the stakes unstated.
- **A plain explanation of FBA.** The material describes the family only in passing ("stoichiometric LP optimization", models "calibrated to maximize biomass production or energy substrate uptake", fluxes "balanced against an electron donor pool"). It does not explain the steady-state assumption, what constraints/bounds are, or what an exchange reaction is. I built the explainer from those fragments and kept it thin rather than add outside knowledge.
- **How a kinetic model works in more than one line.** Only "rate-equation integration (kinetic/ODE family)" is given. Nothing on what parameters such models need, what data they are fitted to, or their costs relative to FBA. The comparison section is therefore lopsided: the material says what each family resolves, not what each costs or needs.
- **What "cybernetic" means.** Song & Liu's models are named and said to have "dynamic regulation", but the approach is not explained. I described them only by that.
- **Trade-offs stated by the sources themselves.** The comparison of strengths and weaknesses across the three families is mostly my synthesis of what each family has or has not been used for; the material gives a verdict (which family does what) rather than a stated comparison of merits.
- **Whether A. tumefaciens has NosZ.** The Kampschreur model is listed with Nap/NirK/NorB only. I could not tell whether the organism lacks *nosZ* (which would make it another "terminal N₂O" case) or whether the model just omitted it, so I did not say.
- **Details of the electron-competition models.** Attributed to "Pan et al., ES&T" with no year and no entry in the reference list. I described them without a citation.
- **Mass/charge balancing in the Mellbye reconstructions.** The material says this could not be confirmed, so I did not describe how the N-oxide reactions are coupled to electron carriers in those models.
- **Why FBA struggles with by-product overflow.** Stated as "a general limitation noted across the byproduct-secretion modelling literature" with no source or mechanism. I reported it as a limitation without explaining it.

## 2. Things in the material that were hard to use

- **Written for a project, not a reader.** The note opens with an integration callout about "H2 §2.4", "[[Community FBA]]", "[[Dual Readout]]" and "the user's 'community-level pathway gaps' mechanism". The verdict ("PARTIALLY DEMONSTRATED") and the benchmark list are framed against that project's claim. I had to strip the framing and recast the gap as a general "what has not been published".
- **Internal inconsistency on the 60% figure.** The text says the 2016 model found that "NO oxidation to NO2(-) decreased N2O formation by 60%"; the JSON index says "CytP460 reduces N2O by 60%". I used the text's verbatim quote and did not attribute it to cytochrome P460.
- **Mellbye model type is labelled two ways.** The text calls it genome-scale constraint-based; the JSON calls it "genome-scale dynamic FBA". Since the caveats say dynamic FBA blurs the constraint-based/kinetic line, this matters for the classification and is not resolved.
- **Validation figure lacks context.** "Deviating on average by 15% from values for 17 M yield ratios" contains a stray "M" that I could not interpret; I reported it as 17 measured yield ratios.
- **References missing for cited works.** iMO1056 (Oberhardt et al., 2008), Pan et al. (electron competition), Domingo-Félez & Smets (NDHA, acronym never expanded), ASM3 extensions and AGORA/AGORA2 are mentioned in the text but absent from the reference list. Three listed references (11, 13, 14) are flagged as having truncated author lists.
- **iGC535 accuracy claim is muddled.** "Over 90% prediction accuracy (predicting growth and oxygen uptake rates with 90.52% accuracy...)" and the inference that it shows "considerable manual curation effort" is the reviewer's reading rather than a reported finding. I used it lightly and softened it to "suggests".
- **Negative claims rest on a search, not on the papers.** Many statements are of the form "no instance located"; they are bounded to June 2026 and to a "thorough but finite" search. I kept that caveat in the piece but could not give the reader a sense of how wide the search was, because the material does not describe it.
- **Bold-labelled lists and a JSON block** repeat the same facts three or four times (TL;DR, Key Findings, Details, Verdict, JSON) with small wording differences, which made it slow to tell which version was authoritative when they diverged.
