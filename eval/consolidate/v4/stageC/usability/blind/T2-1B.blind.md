Errors (quote each sentence):
- "Features were hidden one entry at a time, at random, not a whole node at once, and they were sparse word counts." Not all Feature Propagation benchmarks used sparse word counts. OGBN-Arxiv has dense averaged word embeddings, and PubMed uses TF-IDF. Minor overgeneralisation.
- "APPNP is mathematically a random walk that periodically restarts at its origin, so a path through a shared intermediary does raise the similarity between A and B." Personalised PageRank restarts at random, with a fixed probability at each step, not periodically. Minor.
- "Yun, S. et al. (2024). Oldie but Goodie: Re-illuminating Label Propagation on Graphs with Partially Observed Features. arXiv:2508.01209." An arXiv ID beginning 2508 dates to August 2025, which doesn't fit a 2024 year. "Yun et al. (2024)" in the text inherits the inconsistency. Minor citation error.
- Possible misattribution, not counted: "Rossi et al. (2022) describe this effect for zero-filling". The mechanism of shrinking towards the origin is Yi et al.'s analysis. Rossi et al. show zero-filling underperforming but may not describe this mechanism.
- Imprecision, not counted: "70% the average of its neighbours" (APPNP uses normalised adjacency, not a plain mean), and "any step that rescales magnitudes, such as centring or whitening" (centring shifts vectors rather than rescaling them).
