# Tier 4 narrated animation: what the loop did

Rebuilt 2026-10-20 from research, not memory: the MB-01 literature review (vault `Topics/Biology/Research/Results`) supplied every fact; the earlier version (12 segments, 2 min 32 s) skipped every step between sequencing and taxonomic assignment. Now 21 segments in 7 chapters (Soil, Genes, Amplify, Sequence, Reads, Depth, Rarefy), 5 min 6 s.

- **What the research changed:** extraction added as a distorting step; copy number 1 to 15 (was 1 to 10); PCR bias calibrated to the one measured spread (19% of taxa beyond fourfold after 35 cycles, Silverman 2021) instead of an invented per-cycle efficiency; reads per sample differ up to about 100-fold within a study; indexing, demultiplexing, merging and filtering, denoising, chimeras and the classifier each get a segment; the ending says rarefying fixes depth and nothing else, that bias can differ between samples, that the rarefying debate is open, and that shares cannot show load. Dropped: "thousands of cells in a droplet" and "millions of copies after 25 cycles" (no source).
- **Blind fact-check** (../factcheck.md): 1 unsupported (m9 merged two experiments into one figure), 6 overstated (m7, m8, m17, m25, m27, m29: qPCR named where the sources used flow cytometry and spike-ins). All fixed before the final render.
- **Round 1 reader**, blocking: labels under arrows (s6), unlabelled read bars (s12), a legend for errors no longer on screen (s13), the detector on the caption and uncaptioned crosses (s14), ticks skipping the two numbers that matter (s19). Fixed.
- **Round 2 reader**, blocking: the random draw from pool to sequencer was never drawn (s10). Fixed, with deepest-first rows (s16), axis titles (s17), "exaggerated here" on the misassigned read (s11).

Fixed in the final render (one schematic depth shared by s10 and s15).
