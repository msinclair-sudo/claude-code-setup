**D1. Manufacturing energy and emissions, and emission share**

*Answer, as the research gave it; not checked by a second reader.* **Question 1 — Manufacturing energy and emissions, and emission share: WELL-ESTABLISHED WITH QUANTIFIED FIGURES**

Independent industrial-ecology and biogeochemistry sources converge. Manufacture costs about 28 to 37 GJ and emits about 1.6 to 2.8 t CO₂ per tonne of ammonia, equivalently about 1.9 to 3.4 t CO₂-equivalent per tonne of N (Tock et al. 2015; Demirhan et al. 2019; Razon 2014; Tunå et al. 2014). Manufacture is about 0.9% to 1.8% of global emissions; the synthetic-N supply chain about 2.1% of global and 10.6% of agricultural emissions (Menegat et al. 2022); the full life cycle including manure about 5% of global emissions (Gao and Cabrera Serrenho 2023).

**D2. Does microbial nitrogen cycling reduce synthetic input?**

*Answer, as the research gave it; not checked by a second reader.* **Question 2 — Microbial cycling reduces synthetic input: SPLIT**

For deliberate legume-based biological fixation, the reduction is ESTABLISHED BUT QUALIFIED, with strong direct evidence bounded by wide variability in fixation fraction and by fertiliser suppression of fixation (Anglade et al. 2015; Ladha et al. 2022). For the resident microbial community cycling nutrients more efficiently, as the question is literally posed, the link is WEAKLY / ONLY INDIRECTLY SUPPORTED. The inoculant evidence measures yield gain rather than input reduction at constant yield (INDIRECT; Schütz et al. 2018), free-living fixation is modest (INDIRECT; Ladha et al. 2022), and mineralisation rarely meets high-yield demand (St. Luce et al. 2011). No reviewed source isolates microbial-community efficiency from confounding soil and management variables as the cause of a reduced synthetic requirement at held yield.

### symbiotic biological nitrogen fixation by legumes and rhizobia

  Holds for: cropping systems.

  Holds for: an organic legume-based rotation in Europe, against fertilised systems; N surplus in kg N per hectare per year.
- The mechanism is direct: fixed nitrogen substitutes for applied nitrogen in the legume and partially enriches the following crop. (the review cites no source for this sentence)
  Holds for: legume symbiotic fixation and the following crop.

### microbial inoculants (biofertilisers)

- Microbial inoculants raise yield inconsistently: the largest meta-analysis, spanning 171 field studies, found yield responses of +8.5% in continental climates, +10.0% in oceanic, +14.9% in tropical and +20.0% in dry climates, with effects described as often inconsistent and dependent on soil phosphorus and climate. (Schutz et al. 2018)
  Holds for: a meta-analysis of 171 field studies; yield response by climate (continental, oceanic, tropical, dry); dependent on soil phosphorus and climate.
- The inoculant evidence is INDIRECT for the question of reduced synthetic input, because it measures yield gain at a given input rather than input reduction at a held yield. (Schutz et al. 2018)
  Holds for: inoculant field evidence; yield gain at a given input, not input reduction at a held yield.
