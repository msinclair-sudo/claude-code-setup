Errors (quote each sentence):
- "Genome-scale models exist for several organisms that do run the whole pathway: *Paracoccus denitrificans* (the textbook complete denitrifier, with Nap, NirS, Nor and NosZ), ..." P. denitrificans carries both the membrane-bound Nar and the periplasmic Nap; Nar is the main respiratory nitrate reductase in anaerobic denitrification, so listing Nap alone misdescribes the textbook organism.
- "FBA needs a genome and a stoichiometric network but no rates, and it gives a distribution of fluxes." Overstated: FBA needs only a stoichiometric network (plus bounds, which are often measured uptake rates); the piece's own Perez-Garcia 2014 example is a 51-reaction energy-metabolism network, not a genome-scale reconstruction.
- Possible inconsistency (not counted): "It is the only community-scale constraint-based model found that reports N₂O." sits two paragraphs after the 2016 follow-up "widened the network to eight species of ammonia and nitrite oxidisers", which reports an N₂O reduction; the reader can't tell whether the 2016 model was a community model.
- Soft overstatement (not counted): "Nobody has published that." flat absence claim, qualified only later in the closing paragraph.

Notes:
- Mechanistic contrast (overflow valve vs throttled four-step chain) is the piece's main insight and is well made.
- Several numbers (51 reactions/44 metabolites, within 15% of 17 yield ratios, 60% reduction) could not be checked from my own knowledge; reported as the authors' results, not flagged as errors.
- Source list is present but in-text naming is uneven; a reader can't map "a heavily re-curated N. europaea model from 2022" or the Agrobacterium model to a source without guessing.
