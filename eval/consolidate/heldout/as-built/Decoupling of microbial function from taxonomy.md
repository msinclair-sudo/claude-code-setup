### n-cycle-hgt: Horizontal transfer of nitrogen-cycle genes
needs: hgt
- **n-cycle-hgt.1** Heylen et al. (2006), analysing 227 cultivated denitrifiers, found nirK phylogeny incongruent with 16S rRNA phylogeny, with nirK sequences significantly more similar within habitats than within closely related taxa, which the review reads as direct evidence that nirK has moved horizontally across lineages under shared environmental selection.
  conf: not stated · class: not stated · holds: 227 cultivated denitrifiers · src: heylen2006 · trail: H1-RES §Sub-claim 3 · from: H1-RES:B-0103, H1-RES:B-0104 · rating: "The most compelling quantitative evidence"
