# Notes on writing piece.md

## 1. Wanted or needed, and not in the material

- **Why N₂O matters.** Nothing says why anyone wants N₂O predicted (greenhouse gas, ozone, emissions from agriculture or wastewater). An outside reader needs this as motivation. I left it out and opened on the pathway, which makes the opening weaker.
- **Why biomass-maximising FBA won't release a byproduct.** The material states the limitation ("does not naturally predict byproduct overflow without additional constraints") but gives no reason and no reference. I wanted one sentence of explanation for a non-specialist and had nothing to base it on.
- **Electrons per step.** Only the total is given (10 electrons per two nitrate to N₂). Per-step counts would have made the electron-overflow argument concrete.
- **How the Perez-Garcia and Mellbye models actually force N₂O out.** The overflow mechanism is described, but not what constraint or objective in the FBA produces it (an oxygen uptake cap? a different objective?). The material says the reaction stoichiometry and electron coupling could not be checked. This is the core "how" of the piece and it is thin.
- **What the 2016 network's N₂O output looks like.** Whether the eight-species network reports N₂O as a distinct flux is stated inconsistently (see below), so I described only the 60% simulation result.
- **How kinetic and cybernetic models work.** Beyond "rate-equation integration" and "dynamic regulation", there is nothing on what a cybernetic model is or how it differs from dynamic FBA. I gave a one-clause description of kinetic models and none of cybernetic ones.
- **Electron-competition models.** No author list, year or details beyond one sentence (Pan et al., ES&T). Same for the Domingo-Félez and Smets NDHA model (no year, journal or what NDHA stands for), so I dropped NDHA from the piece.
- **Strengths of FBA relative to kinetic models.** The material is all about what FBA lacks for N₂O. Nothing on what FBA gains (no kinetic parameters needed, genome-scale coverage), so the comparison is one-sided. I did not supply this from outside.
- **How the Zakem model performs.** No result is given (e.g. whether it reproduces observed N₂O in oxygen minimum zones), only its structure.
- **What "17 M yield ratios" means.** The quote says "17 M yield ratios"; the "M" is unexplained. I wrote "17 published yield ratios".
- **Full references** for Oberhardt et al. (2008), Pan et al. (ES&T), Domingo-Félez and Smets, AGORA/AGORA2, CarveMe and the byproduct-secretion literature are missing; Yuan, Dahal and Zakem have abbreviated author lists. The piece's reference list flags the two I cite without full details.

## 2. Hard to use in the material

- **Inconsistency on which FBA models emit N₂O.** The verdict names Perez-Garcia 2014, 2016 and Mellbye 2018; the recommendations say only 2014 and 2018; the model index marks 2016 as N₂O-explicit. I avoided a count and described 2016 by its simulation result.
- **The 60% attribution.** One place says NO oxidation to nitrite cut N₂O by 60%, the model index says CytP460 does. I used the quoted version and added a parenthetical that the two may be the same finding, reasoning from the 2014 statement that P460 oxidises NO. That reconciliation is mine, not the material's.
- **Mellbye classification.** Described as constraint-based in the title of the paper and as "genome-scale dynamic FBA" in the model index, while the review says dynamic FBA blurs the constraint-based/kinetic boundary. It was unclear how much weight "dynamic" should carry; I mentioned it once.
- **Heavy repetition.** The same verdict appears four or five times in near-identical long sentences (verdict, two concept bullets, "Do not say", "Say instead"). Finding the distinct facts took sifting.
- **Leftover framing from the original brief.** References to "the user's mechanism" and "community-level pathway gaps" as if the reader knew the brief; I had to infer what the user's hypothesis was (N₂O as truncation from missing/capped nosZ or a community gap).
- **Unsourced background.** The pathway, enzymes and the 10-electron figure carry "the note cites no source". I used them as standard background without a citation.
- **Zakem duplicate.** The bioRxiv preprint and PNAS paper are listed as two references with the same title; I cited the PNAS version only.
- **Mixed nomenclature.** "Nor", "cNor/qNor", "norBC", "NorB" and "NOR-encoding gene" are used interchangeably; I settled on Nor/*norBC* in the pathway and NorB where the source names it.
- **No second reader.** The material says its findings were never checked by a second reader, so I could not tell how much to trust the synthesised claims (e.g. the "overflow valve" framing) versus the quoted ones.
