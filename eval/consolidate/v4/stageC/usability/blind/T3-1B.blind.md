Errors (quote each sentence):
- "Each becomes its own reaction, drawing electrons from a shared donor pool (NADH, quinones) and coupled to proton translocation." Overstated: Nar contributes to the proton motive force through its quinol-oxidising redox loop, but NirK/NirS, cNor and NosZ are periplasmic or non-electrogenic and do not translocate protons themselves; proton translocation occurs upstream (NADH dehydrogenase, bc1 complex). Stated for every step, it is wrong.
- Possible, not counted: "(One account credits the 60% to cytochrome P460, the NO-oxidising enzyme in the 2014 model; ...)" Cytochrome P460 in N. europaea is better known (Caranto et al. 2016) for converting hydroxylamine to N2O; calling it "the NO-oxidising enzyme" may reflect the 2014 model's assumption rather than established biochemistry. The phrase "in the 2014 model" partly protects it.
- Possible, not counted: "Community models report aggregate capability and the fluxes exchanged between members, not resolved sub-steps; ..." generalisation about all community models, at odds with the piece's own Mellbye 2018 example, which resolves NO, NO2 and N2O.
