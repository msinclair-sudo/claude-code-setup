# Notes

## 1. Wanted or needed for the piece, not found in the material

- **What a ghost actually is in the pipeline.** The material never says why these nodes lack features: whether they are cited works outside the harvested corpus, papers without abstracts, or something else. I wrote around it ("they sit in the network because of their links"). An outside reader will want to know.
- **What "degree >= 2" counts.** Links in total, citations received, or co-citing papers? I said "at least two links", which may be imprecise.
- **What the current imputed anchor is.** The text calls it "imputed-anchor design" and "the diffusion average" but never states the procedure (mean of neighbours, FP reconstruction, something else). I hedged with "typically a diffusion-style average".
- **Whether the pipeline's 100-d space is PCA output.** "Fused 100-d space" and "PCA at 100-d" both appear; I assumed the same 100 dimensions, which the material implies but does not state.
- **Any measurement on the pipeline itself.** No numbers on ghost-vector variance, bridge survival, or cluster quality. Every claim about this pipeline is extrapolation, so the piece cannot report a single observed effect.
- **The size of the 60% crossover effect.** Rossi et al. say the gap "opens up" but the material gives no accuracy figures for the 60-90% band, only the 99% headline.
- **What the Feature Propagation 4.12% drop is relative to,** and on which datasets.
- **Whether the trade-off from raising the threshold is large.** Only one ghost/real count (at >= 2) is given; no counts at >= 3 or >= 5, so I could not show how fast the ghost share falls.
- **Definitions for an outsider:** what UMAP's min_dist/n_neighbours and HDBSCAN's mutual-reachability mean (I dropped these), what MADGap and Dirichlet energy measure, and what PCFI is beyond one line. I left these out rather than define them from outside knowledge.

- **Mis-attributed link.** The oversmoothing sentence cites Cai & Wang and Oono & Suzuki but links arXiv 2601.07419, which matches neither bibliography entry (2006.13318 and Oono's ICLR paper).
- **Date mismatch for Yun et al.** Cited as 2024 in text and BibTeX, but the arXiv ID 2508.01209 implies 2025. I avoided giving a year.
- **FSD-CAP 2026** appears only in the Caveats as a recent preprint; it is never described in the body and has no citation entry.
- **"Deep bibliographic coupling work"** is invoked as evidence for Q1 with no author, year or citation.
- **Um et al. 2025 is called a preprint in Caveats** but cited as an ICML 2025 paper elsewhere. I wrote "recent and little replicated" rather than "preprints".
