# Grading rubric for the writing stage (recorded, not applied)

From the owner's grading of the v3 usability pieces (2026-10-22). It grades a piece written from a knowledge
note or its writer's view. Not applied until the writing skill exists, including in the v4 usability re-test.

Score each item 0 (absent or wrong), 1 (partly), 2 (done throughout). Each item also counts its failures, so a
piece can be compared on counts as well as scores.

| # | Item | 2 means | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Citations and references** | every finding attributed in text (author, year), and a reference list covering every source cited | findings without attribution; cited sources missing from the list |
| 2 | **Dense numbers in tables or figures** | wherever three or more figures are compared, they are in a table or figure, each with its unit and source | dense comparisons left in prose |
| 3 | **Scope and breadth** | each claim says the setting it holds for and how far to take it (one study, one system, simulated, a review's corpus) | claims stated more widely than their setting |
| 4 | **Absences qualified by search status** | every "untested", "not found", "nobody has" says which: searched and not found, not searched, or not stated by the research | absences stated as fact about the literature |
| 5 | **Opposing positions searched** | before a conclusion, the piece says whether contrary evidence was looked for, and what was found | conclusions with no statement about contrary evidence |
| 6 | **Ambiguity flagged** | where the research is ambiguous or conflicting, the piece says so as ambiguity, not as a resolved point | ambiguous points stated as settled |

Not graded here: style, narrative structure, length. Facts are graded by the fact-check against the knowledge
note, not by this rubric. Later, with biblion: whether each gap names the closest literature to it.
