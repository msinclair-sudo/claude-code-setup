- **Rules into the skill** (storyboard.md "On-screen text has a job" and "Explaining terms and charts"; t4-animation.md continuity, sync, fast text; review.md checklist): text labels a thing, gives a number or term, or names the step; new terms defined in the subject's terms; a renamed thing keeps its old name; a chart's finding first, then its axes; relative change shown directly; introduce before use; the field's usual plot.
- **Changes:** step headings at top left replace narration-echo captions; three taxa's 16S genes show conserved stretches alike and the variable region different, primers land on all three, the barcode is braced as "a variable region (V4) of the 16S rRNA gene"; PCR bias is a schematic race plus one study's figure, then the simulated dot plot with its axis drawn and said first; taxon 1 against taxon 3 lifts out into a two-bar chart whose ratio goes 3, 53, 85 to 1, with the history kept; sequencing opens on soil → cell → DNA → barcode copies dropping into sample A's tube; meters grow as each read lands; the read pair is introduced at the sequencer; merging shows the overlap; filtering is a read-length histogram with a cut-off (illustrative); the closing limits are pictures (bias arrow, missed slots, two soils with the same shares), not sentences.

- **Wording:** spoken sentences of about 20 words; "we" and "you"; "abundant" and "low-abundance" for counts in one sample (MB-02: the field splits on "rare", so avoid it); taxa defined when first said; copy number in two segments (per genome, 1 to about 15, rarely more; per cell, from one to dozens in cultures, barely measured in soil); real-soil evidence for the long tail (2% of taxa, about 41% of reads).
- **Motion:** fades went from 119 of 170 animations to 30 (exits only). Things that become other things are transformed (a cell's DNA into a ring, the same bar chart reshaped at each chapter's end); pictures are drawn up (Create, Write, GrowFromEdge); reads travel along paths into the sequencer; the camera pushes into the droplet.
