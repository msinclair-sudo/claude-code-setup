**Seventh version (2026-10-21), after the owner's review of the sixth:** "'the truth an experiment sets out to measure': awful wording"; "then we go straight into 'first we pull the DNA', way too abrupt... there's no connection between counting taxa and extracting DNA. There's a question missing: how do we count the taxa in the first place? The narrative still has too many gaps."

- **Narrative read:** a fresh reader given only the narration, audience and message (no pictures) listed about 25 gaps, a step arriving without the question that motivates it, plus wording faults. Taken: nearly all; not taken: axes before the finding (s20d, against the owner's rule) and swapping depth and demultiplexing (the meters fill as reads are sequenced, the owner's earlier request).
- **Bridges:** s5a (we can't count bacteria by eye, so we read their DNA, first extracting it), s11a (we still have to pick the 16S gene out and copy it: PCR does both), s16b (read pairs, split from s16 so the random draw gets its reason: the pool holds far more copies than a run reads). Every chapter's first segment carries a `question:`; the openings now name it (s15 "So far we've followed one sample", s25 "Now, what does depth itself do?", s29 "That's a problem when we compare samples"). Wording: "extract", not "pull"; s4 "Those true counts are what we want to measure"; taxon 1 and taxon 3 introduced in s3; PCR bias named; primers defined; read defined; the barcode names the taxon, the index the sample.
- **Rules into the skill:** storyboard.md "every step answers a question the viewer now has" with the `question:` field (`x lint` warns on a chapter without one); review.md step 5, the narrative reader, every round the narration changes.
- **Blind fact-check** of the new claims (m39 to m42, m15 amended): m40 and m41 supported; fixed: 16S tells "most" bacteria apart (s7), depth varies from uneven pooling and uneven DNA yields (s17), misassignment comes from index mix-ups or spillover between wells and "almost none" was a filtered non-16S figure (s18, dropped), "can start out undercounted" (s6), the rarefying dispute is whether the trade is worth it (s33).

**Fifth version (2026-10-21), after the owner's review of the fourth:** "the narration speed is faster than the slides and animations once we get to the PCR section"; back in sync by indexing, out again in merging. Read filtering "should be stated why it's done, not just that it is done".

- **Cause of the drift:** segment boundaries matched the audio within 0.6 s; the drift was inside segments. Fixed run_times (0.5 to 2 s) bunched each segment's motion at its start, so the voice named things seconds before or after they moved (s20 merged the reads at 3 s, while "joined into one merged read" was spoken at 7 s). And the 0.7 s sentence pause had never been applied: kokoro-onnx places it only where the model reports timings, and v1.0 reports none (measured gaps 0.3 to 0.5 s).
- **Tool change:** `tts` speaks one sentence at a time and inserts the pause itself, recording each sentence's span (`<clip>.spans.json`); `ExplainScene.at(t, k[, word])` holds a move until its sentence (or a word in it) is spoken, `left(t, k[, word])` gives the run_time to end there. A cue more than 1 s late is a fault, 0.4 s a warning; a multi-sentence segment with no cue is a warning. Every segment of the exemplar is now cued (88 cues).
- **Read filtering** is now four segments (s20b to s20e): the expected length and taxa differing by a few bases (DADA2 tutorial V4 data: distinct sequences 251 to 255 bases); why reads come out other lengths (quality trimming at read ends; off-target copies from mispriming); the histogram, finding first; the cut-offs as a judgement, usually the same for every sample, with the strict/loose trade-off shown by moving the line. Rule added to storyboard.md: a step is explained by its reason.
- **Checks:** no late cue, no uncued segment; every frame of the new segments checked by eye (s20c's stacked off-target copies and s20e's crowded labels fixed). **Open fault: 10 min 31 s, over the 10-minute cap**, from the real sentence pauses (about 20 s) and the four filtering segments (about 50 s); left for the owner (split into two videos, cut a segment, or accept).
- **Blind fact-check** (m36 to m38): references right; three overstatements fixed in the narration ("can make" for mispriming, "on Illumina machines, quality tends to fall", "usually" for the same cut-offs in every sample); the 251 to 255 counts are distinct sequences, not reads.
